Sentiment analysis of Amazon product reviews across product categories. Pandas and PySpark clean the reviews, and VADER scores their sentiment. Naive Bayes models then predict sentiment, star rating, and category.

### amazon-reviews-nlp-sentiment-analysis

url: https://github.com/t-shah02/amazon-reviews-nlp-sentiment-analysis
stack: Python, PySpark, Pandas, spaCy, Jupyter

ETL scripts, exploratory notebooks, and significance tests. archive.org hosts the cleaned datasets and trained models.
